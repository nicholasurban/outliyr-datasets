# Light Therapy Devices Dataset

76 light-therapy devices (red/NIR panels, wearables, brain PBM, full-spectrum and SAD lamps) with pricing, specs, and instrument measurements where independently verified.

**Trust tiers:** `verified` = tested hands-on by Outliyr with calibrated instruments (Hopoocolor spectroradiometer, flicker analysis, EMF meters) per the published [testing methodology](https://outliyr.com/light-therapy-testing-methodology). `spec-sheet` = manufacturer-claimed data, with claim source URL and capture date recorded for auditability. `community` = user-submitted.

**Key columns:** claimed vs measured irradiance (mW/cm² at 6/12/18 inches; 12 inches is the realistic use distance), wavelengths (nm), flicker index/percent, EMF (electric V/m, magnetic mG at contact and 6 in), dirty electricity delta, power draw, noise.

Live sortable version: https://outliyr.com/app/light-therapy-comparison
